Known deviations
================

This is a public-equivalent ImageNet-1k implementation, not an exact reproduction.

- The paper does not release code, the exact EfficientNet-L2 checkpoint revision, student initialization, `L_in` identities, optimizer, schedule, augmentation, temperature, seed, or latency protocol.

- The teacher is Timm's PyTorch port `tf_efficientnet_l2.ns_jft_in1k_475`, rather than the authors' exact TensorFlow checkpoint. Teacher inputs use that checkpoint's Timm configuration: bicubic resize to 507px followed by center crop to 475px; logits are cached under a versioned configuration-specific path.

- Table 4 scripts use the 300 largest ImageNet training folders as `L_in`, breaking ties by synset. The paper's 300 synsets are undisclosed, so this is only a deterministic public proxy.

- The Table 4 student is Timm `mobilenetv3_large_075`, randomly initialized and trained for 30 epochs with AdamW (initial `lr=1e-3`, default weight decay), cosine decay to `1e-5`, basic crop/flip augmentation, no seed, and temperature 1. Figure 7 scripts retain their earlier 10-epoch constant-rate recipe. These choices are not reported by the paper.

- Table 4 scripts implement baseline, CD-I (`alpha=0.0`, `0.4`, `0.6`), and CD-III. Baseline metrics use `student_only`; CD-I/CD-III metrics use `class_delegation`.

- Figure 7 scripts implement margin-based distillation with `rho_train=0.4` and `0.6`. In-domain reporting uses a fixed teacher margin >= 0.4, following Section 5.2; Figure 7's caption instead says student margin >= 0.4.

- Each training run records epoch training loss and validation student top-1 accuracy in `history.json` and final `metrics.json`. Per-epoch validation is a reporting addition.

- Local latency is a warm GPU forward-pass estimate only. It excludes preprocessing, I/O, routing, and remote teacher transport and is not comparable to the paper's latency measurements.
